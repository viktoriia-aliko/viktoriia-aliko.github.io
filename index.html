About

Viktoriia Aliko is a freelance model and content creator based in Milan.
Her work spans commercial and beauty campaigns, runway and alternative fashion projects, and event content for brands.

On the runway she has walked for designer Omar Mansoor and for EKO, and in 2026 she worked as a hostess at the BYD stand at bus trade shows in Milan and Madrid.

As a UGC creator she produces content for beauty brands of the L'Oréal Group through a creator platform, and covers launches, brand activations and fashion events in Milan and across Europe with ready-to-post photo and video for social media.
